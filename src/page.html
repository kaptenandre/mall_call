      Palette
      

        
Drag any of these onto the timeline: it lands where you drop it and snaps to the nearest hit. Drag a look onto a part to give that part the look. A press, or the key, drops it at the playhead instead.

      

    


    

      Logo file
      
      
      
Showing a placeholder ring. Only the shape is used, so a one-colour logo on a transparent background works best.

      
    


    

      Sound
      
      
      
      
No sound yet. The piece runs as a seamless loop on a built-in half-second beat.

      
    


    

      Symbols
      

      
      
      
A bank of shapes. Press one to put it on the timeline at the playhead; the plus adds one of your own. A Symbol clip can walk through the bank on every hit, with echoes behind it showing the shapes that came before.

    


    

      Pillars
      
      
Patterns that play across the pillars so they stop doing the same thing: chases, spins, pops on the hit, a pillar per band of the sound. A pattern either lights whole pillars or shows the picture only where it is on. This sets how often the automatic cut uses them. Your own go on the Pillars lane.

    


    

      1 Base layer
      
Big and rare: a wall of light sweeping through, a full strobe, a flood, a blackout. Lands on the strongest hits only.

      
      
      
    


    

      2 Glitch layer
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
Pulse lines are short lit stretches that strike out on a hit and sink back, like a lamp. Slide lines run the full frame and ease from place to place. Blocks, bars and sparks each listen to their own part of the spectrum. Auto clips is how many clips the automatic cut puts on the glitch lane: chases through a build, lines racing into a drop, bursts where a band jumps. Wobble is how far the picture tears and slips, from none to always moving; size runs from tight ripples to long waves.

      4 Type layer
      
Words are a layer of their own, on top, and only there while a Type clip plays. They keep clear of the glitch unless Warp lets it in. How a clip arrives is chosen on the clip.

      
      
      
    


    

      Variation
      
      
      
      
      
      
      

      
At once is how much may happen at the same time: lower it and the debris, the chases and the automatic clips all thin out together. Easing sets how much everything that moves surges on a hit and settles after it, instead of running at one speed. A seed always gives the same cut, so note the number when you like one.

    


    

      Pixels
      

        
        
        
      

      
      
      
Dither type applies to any layer drawn as Dither. Pixel size is set per layer.

      Timecode
      
      
      
      
The timecode the first frame of the piece sits on. Set it to the cue time on the lighting desk and the ruler reads the same numbers as the desk.

    


    

      Player
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
For playing straight out of this app. Music goes to the output you choose. Timecode can be sent as sound (LTC) for a desk to follow, or read from an input so the piece follows the desk. Set the rate and the start under Timecode.

    


    

      Output
      
      
      
      
      
      
The last thing done to the picture, in the preview, the output window and every export. For a see-through LED mesh: raise Contrast and Black floor so dim pixels go fully dark instead of glowing grey, and lower Gamma to lift what is left.

      
      
      
      
A clean window with only the picture, for a second screen. Double-click it for full screen.

    


    

      Export
      
      
      
      
      

      
Every frame is rendered once at full size. Without sound the file loops seamlessly; with sound it runs the length of the track and carries the audio. ProRes and HAP also carry the start timecode.
