Logo file

Showing a placeholder ring. Only the shape is used, so a one-colour logo on a transparent background works best.

Sound

No sound yet. The piece runs as a seamless loop on a built-in half-second beat.

1 Base layer

Big and rare: a wall of light sweeping through, a full strobe, a flood, a blackout. Lands on the strongest hits only.

2 Glitch layer

Lines carry the fast ease in and out. Blocks, bars and sparks each listen to their own part of the spectrum. Auto clips is how many clips the automatic cut puts on the glitch lane: chases through a build, lines racing into a drop, bursts where a band jumps. Wobble is how far the picture tears and slips, from none to always moving; size runs from tight ripples to long waves.

Variation

Easing sets how much everything that moves surges on a hit and settles after it, instead of running at one speed. A seed always gives the same cut, so note the number when you like one.
